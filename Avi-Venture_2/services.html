How we support your wedding journey

From first idea to final farewell, we offer layered support. You can choose full planning or specific services depending on how hands-on your family wants to be.

01 · End-to-end

Complete Wedding Planning

Ideal for couples and families who want a single team to lead everything. We handle:

  • Budget planning and high-level wedding strategy.
  • Venue research, shortlisting and finalisation.
  • Vendor recommendations, negotiations and coordination.
  • Ceremony flow, logistics, guest experience and hospitality.
  • On-ground team for multi-day execution.

This is our most immersive service and includes regular check-in calls and a shared planning dashboard.

02 · Multi-day

Multi-day & Multi-venue Planning

For weddings spread across several days and locations, we design:

  • Guest movement plans between venues and hotels.
  • Event blocks that balance rituals, rest and celebration.
  • Vendor schedules that minimise overlaps and delays.
  • Detailed contingency plans for weather and traffic.

Your guests feel they are simply flowing through a story — not constantly checking WhatsApp groups for what is happening next.

03 · Traditions

Ritual & Ceremony Guidance

Many couples know certain rituals matter to their families, but not always what they mean or how long they take. We:

  • Speak with priests/elders to understand expectations.
  • Map each ritual and its approximate duration.
  • Create an easy-to-read ceremony guide for both families.
  • Help integrate cross-cultural or interfaith elements with sensitivity.
04 · Coordination

Month-of & Wedding Day Management

For families who have done most of the planning but want a calm, professional team to run the day, we:

  • Review your plans and vendor list 4–6 weeks before.
  • Create detailed timelines and contact sheets.
  • Coordinate with vendors and venue teams.
  • Lead rehearsals and oversee ceremonies and receptions.

Not sure which service you need? Share where you are in your planning journey and we’ll suggest a range that feels realistic for your family.
