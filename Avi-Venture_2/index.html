Our story

Traditional at heart, composed in execution.

Avni Ventures was created to bridge ritual-rich Indian weddings with calm, modern planning.

Why families choose us

Every family carries its own customs, expectations, and ways of celebrating. Our role is to hold that complexity with care and turn it into a wedding where everyone feels seen.

From the first phone call to the moment the last light dims at your reception, our team is quietly orchestrating everything behind the scenes—so you can be fully present with the people you love.

We’re known for:

  • South Indian & pan-Indian weddings where rituals are honoured without feeling rigid.
  • Cross-cultural celebrations balancing two sets of customs and families.
  • Multi-day, multi-venue logistics that feel seamless to your guests.
  • Vendor coordination that respects your budget, values, and aesthetic.

We collaborate with priests, venues, decor, photography, and hospitality partners who understand the emotional gravity of a wedding day—not just its schedule.

Services

How we support your wedding journey

Whether you need end-to-end planning or support closer to the date, we shape our involvement to fit your family’s needs.

01 · Full planning

Complete Wedding Planning

From budgeting and venue scouting to ceremony flow, decor, timelines, and on-ground execution, we handle it all with a single point of calm communication for your family.

02 · Multi-day

Multi-Day & Multi-Venue Weddings

Ideal for celebrations that unfold across several days and locations. We synchronise logistics, rituals, guest movement, and vendors so each event feels intentional—not chaotic.

03 · Traditions

Rituals & Tradition Guidance

We help families align on ceremony order, meanings, and timings by working with priests and elders—so everyone knows what to expect and can participate with confidence.

04 · Coordination

Wedding Day Coordination

For couples who’ve planned most pieces and want an experienced team to run the day: vendor handling, timeline management, guest assistance, and last-minute troubleshooting.
