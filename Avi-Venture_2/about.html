About

Traditional at heart, composed in execution.

Avni Ventures is a wedding planning studio based in Bangalore, focused on ritual-rich South Indian and cross–cultural weddings. We balance deep respect for tradition with calm, modern coordination.

How we work

We approach every wedding like a bespoke project. No two families have the same rituals, dynamics, or expectations — so we start by listening. We map out your ceremonies, family preferences, constraints, and dreams before we speak about decor or vendors.

From there, we build a detailed wedding blueprint: guest journeys, time-lines, logistics plans, and ceremony flow. This blueprint guides vendors, venues and our own team so everyone is aligned on the same vision and rhythm.

On the days of the wedding, we operate quietly in the background — troubleshooting, coordinating and prompting — so you and your family can simply experience the moments.

What matters to us

Three principles shape every wedding we plan: respect for ritual, emotional safety for families, and thoughtful, guest–friendly logistics.

01 · Ritual

Respecting tradition

We collaborate with priests and elders to understand the spirit behind each ritual, not just the order of steps. This allows us to design timelines that honour the meaning without feeling rushed.

02 · Family

Holding many perspectives

Weddings gather people across generations and geographies. We mediate expectations gently, making sure each stakeholder feels heard while keeping the couple at the centre.

03 · Logistics

Calm, precise execution

Our detailed run-sheets, checklists and vendor coordination frameworks reduce last-minute stress. The goal is simple: every guest feels that “things just happened smoothly”.

04 · Aesthetic

Visual storytelling

We work with decor and photography partners who can translate your story into spaces and images that will age gracefully — elegant now and timeless later.

Founders

The team behind Avni Ventures

Avni Ventures was founded by three friends who grew up around big South Indian weddings and saw both the beauty and the stress families carried.

Aditi Rao

Co-founder · Lead Planner & Ritual Consultant

Aditi leads overall planning, priest coordination and ceremony design. She loves translating complex rituals into simple, clear experiences that guests and the couple can follow.

Meera Nair

Co-founder · Guest Experience & Hospitality

Meera obsessively maps guest journeys — from airport arrivals to room drops and transport. Her focus is making every guest feel gently cared for without overwhelming them with instructions.

Karthik Menon

Co-founder · Operations & Vendor Partnerships

Karthik handles operations, budgets and vendor relationships. He ensures every partner understands the plan, delivers on time, and aligns with the family’s values and priorities.

Together, the three founders personally oversee every wedding Avni Ventures takes on, limiting the number of celebrations each season so they can stay hands-on.
